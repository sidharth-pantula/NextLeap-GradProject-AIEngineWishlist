7. SPECIAL ACTIVATION DEEP-DIVES

Price Dynamics, Festival Curation, Availability & Timing, Confidence Building
sell Price & Sale Dynamics BEHAVIOURAL + VOC

Price Drops Catalyze Purchase ONLY When Fit Is Verified

In analysed behavioural data, price drops correlate with a +23.1% conversion lift (31.2% vs 8.1%). However, customer feedback consistently reveals that users still hesitate during sales if garment dimensions or fabric quality remain unconfirmed.

PM Finding: Discounts alone do not eliminate return fear; combine price alerts with size match proof.
celebration Festival & Occasion Re-activation USER CONVERSATIONS

Pre-Sale Curation Followed by Rapid Checkout Spikes

Shoppers save 5-15 fashion items 2-3 weeks ahead of Diwali, Big Fashion Festival, and EORS. During sale launch, 40%+ are revisited, but stock sell-out in key sizes creates sudden drop-offs.

PM Finding: Event deadlines create authentic purchase urgency when stock and sizing are clear.
inventory_2 Availability & Timing Dynamics OBSERVED BEHAVIOUR

Availability Changes & Event Deadlines Create Authentic Purchase Urgency

When a desired size becomes less available (23.4% immediate urgency reaction) or an upcoming calendar occasion approaches, users move from passive postponement to immediate checkout.

PM Finding: Authentic size availability changes and event deadlines reactivate saved items without needing discounts.
verified Confidence-Building & Peer Proof COMMUNITY VOC

Customer Photos & Reviews Provide Crucial Purchase Confidence

46.8% of users research garment dimensions, 38.3% seek community peer opinions, and 35.1% scrutinize real daylight customer photos to resolve sizing and sheer fabric doubts before checkout.

Key Purchase Enabler: Providing real-world body dimensions and unedited fabric photos removes the primary hesitation barrier.

Research Sources & Methodology

Data Provenance, Epistemic Framework & Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

Data Provenance & Purpose

This engine combines quantitative behavioral research datasets with qualitative public customer feedback to identify and quantify why fashion shoppers save items without converting within 30 days.

query_stats Wishlist Behaviour Research Dataset

Used to analyse wishlist addition timelines, price trajectories, alert adoption, and 30-day conversion patterns (N=5,000 records). Source: electricsheepafrica research dataset.

moving Clickstream Progression Dataset

Used to benchmark general progression from product view to cart and order completion (N=3,949 events). Source: REES46 multi-category e-commerce benchmark.

forum Public User Conversations (Reddit & Web)

Qualitative customer discussions from Indian fashion communities (r/IndianFashionAddicts, r/TwoXIndia) identifying sizing discrepancies and fabric sheerness skepticism (N=243 records).

smart_display YouTube Fashion Community

Verified customer comments on try-on hauls, customer drape, and sizing conversion across major apparel brands (N=66 records).

Integrity & Non-Affiliation Disclaimer: All findings are strictly derived from open research datasets, public forums, and customer conversations. The dataset is used as a research benchmark and is not presented as proprietary first-party telemetry from Myntra, AJIO, or Flipkart.
